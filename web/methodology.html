1. 目標與名詞

我們要回答：「CPI 年增率（或月增率）是多少，其中各細項各貢獻了幾個百分點？」細項的貢獻度取決於兩件事：它在消費籃子裡佔多大（權重），以及它漲了多少。

符號意義
\(I_i(t)\)細項 \(i\) 在第 \(t\) 月的 CPI-U 指數（all items 記為 \(I_{\text{all}}\)）
\(RI_i(t)\)相對重要性：細項 \(i\) 在 \(t\) 月占整體支出的比重（%）。全部互斥細項加總為 100
\(c_i(t)\)貢獻度，單位為百分點（pp）。各項加總 ≈ 總體變動率
權重年 \(y\)使用同一組支出權重的期間：\(y\) 年 1 月到 12 月
錨點 \(a_y\)權重年 \(y\) 的起點，也就是 \(y-1\) 年 12 月

3. CPI 如何加總

CPI 是修正型 Laspeyres（Lowe）指數：固定一籃子數量，用當期價格重新計價。把指數表示成相對於錨點 \(a\) 的比值時，整體等於各互斥細項價格變動的加權平均，權重就是錨點當月的支出比重：

$$\frac{I_{\text{all}}(t)}{I_{\text{all}}(a)} \;=\; \sum_i s_i(a)\,\frac{I_i(t)}{I_i(a)}, \qquad \sum_i s_i(a)=1 \tag{1}$$

其中 \(s_i(a)=RI_i(a)/100\)。式 (1) 在同一個權重年度內對任何互斥且窮盡的細項組合都精確成立（只差指數四捨五入）。跨過權重更新時不成立，這是第 8 節 residual 的主要來源。

4. 相對重要性（權重）

4.1 年度內逐月更新

籃子數量固定，但各細項價格漲跌不同，支出比重會跟著變。從錨點 \(a\) 到 \(t\) 月：

$$RI_i(t) \;=\; RI_i(a)\cdot\frac{I_i(t)/I_i(a)}{I_{\text{all}}(t)/I_{\text{all}}(a)} \tag{2}$$

這是 BLS 標準的價格更新公式。把式 (2) 對所有細項加總，由式 (1) 可知結果仍是 100。

4.2 12 月有兩套權重

每年 12 月同時有兩組相對重要性：

  • 舊基礎 \(RI_i^{\text{old}}(\text{Dec } y)\)：權重年 \(y\) 的權重，用式 (2) 更新到 12 月。
  • 新基礎 \(RI_i^{\text{new}}(\text{Dec } y)\)：權重年 \(y+1\) 的錨點。BLS 每年公布的 12 月表就是這一組。

貢獻度永遠使用基期的權重。12 月當基期時（隔年 1 月的月增率、隔年 12 月的年增率），價格變動發生在權重年 \(y+1\) 內，所以要用新基礎。本專案把每年 12 月的 \(RI\) 一律設為新基礎；少了這一步，12 月的貢獻度就加不起來。

5. 錨點權重從哪來

5.1 官方表（預設）

權重年 \(y\) 的錨點直接取 BLS 的 \(y-1\) 年 12 月表。對每一組「母項 → 子項」，子項在母項內的比重為：

$$s_{i\mid P}(a_y) \;=\; \frac{\widetilde{RI}_i}{\sum_{j\in P}\widetilde{RI}_j}$$

\(\widetilde{RI}\) 是官方表上四捨五入到小數三位的數值。用子項加總（而不是母項本身）當分母，可以吸收四捨五入造成的 ±0.001pp 缺口，讓子項加總剛好等於母項。

5.2 從指數反推（備援）

沒有官方表的年份才使用（例如新一年的 12 月表還沒公布）。式 (1) 對未知的 \(s_i\) 是線性的：令 \(x_{i,t}=I_i(t)/I_i(a)\)、\(b_t=I_P(t)/I_P(a)\)，一年最多 12 條方程式，解一個帶約束的最小平方：

$$\min_{s}\ \sum_{t}\Big(\sum_i s_i\,x_{i,t}-b_t\Big)^2 \quad \text{s.t.}\ \sum_i s_i = 1$$

作法是用 \(s_n = 1-\sum_{i\lt n}s_i\) 消去最後一個未知數，變成一般的最小平方問題：

$$\sum_{i\lt n} s_i\,(x_{i,t}-x_{n,t}) \;=\; b_t - x_{n,t}$$

觀測月數少於未知數時（例如只有 1 月資料），先沿用前一年的錨點比重。

精度限制。2007 年 1 月以前，BLS 指數只公布到小數一位，式 (1) 的殘差從約 \(3\times10^{-6}\) 升到約 \(5\times10^{-4}\)。加上一年內各子項的價格變動高度共線，食物和服務的比重會分不清楚。反推值在 2007 年以後與官方差距多在 ±0.05pp 內，2006 年以前可達 1–4pp。所以反推只當備援，同時也用來檢查官方表解析得對不對（見第 10 節）。

6. 細項層級與組合

權重由以下幾組「互斥且窮盡」的母項 → 子項關係逐層算出：子項權重 ＝ 母項權重 × 子項在母項內的比重。

母項子項
All itemsFood, Energy, Core goods, Core services
All itemsCommodities, Services
FoodFood at home, Food away from home
EnergyEnergy commodities, Energy services
ServicesRent of shelter, Services less rent of shelter

權重和貢獻度都是線性的，所以可以做加減。「核心服務（不含住宅）」就是這樣組出來的：

$$\text{核心服務不含住宅} \;=\; \underbrace{(\text{Services}-\text{Energy services})}_{\text{核心服務}}-\text{Rent of shelter} \;=\; \text{Services less rent of shelter}-\text{Energy services}$$

7. 貢獻度公式

以下 \(RI\) 以 % 表示，貢獻度的單位是百分點。

7.1 月增率（季調）

$$c_i^{\text{MoM}}(t) \;=\; RI_i(t-1)\cdot\left(\frac{I^{SA}_i(t)}{I^{SA}_i(t-1)}-1\right)$$

7.2 年增率（未季調，標準）

$$c_i^{\text{YoY}}(t) \;=\; RI_i(t-12)\cdot\left(\frac{I_i(t)}{I_i(t-12)}-1\right)$$

這是 Bloomberg WMA 的作法，也是儀表板的預設。

7.3 組合細項

組合細項 \(C=\sum_k \sigma_k\,k\)（\(\sigma_k=\pm1\)）的權重與貢獻度都是各節點的帶號加總：\(RI_C=\sum_k\sigma_k RI_k\)，\(c_C=\sum_k\sigma_k c_k\)。

8. Residual 與「精確加總」

8.1 Residual 是什麼

$$r(t) \;=\; \underbrace{\left(\frac{I_{\text{all}}(t)}{I_{\text{all}}(t-h)}-1\right)\times 100}_{\text{公布的總體變動率}} \;-\; \sum_i c_i(t)$$

也就是「各項貢獻加總」和「公布的總體漲幅」的差。例如 2026 年 3 月：3.256 − (0.366 + 0.791 + 0.229 + 1.847) = 0.023。它有三個來源：

  1. 年增率跨過權重更新（主因）。式 (1) 只在同一權重年度內成立。基期 \(t-12\) 與 \(t\) 分屬兩個權重年時，用基期權重乘各項漲幅就加不回總體。反例可以驗證：12 月年增率的基期正好是上一年 12 月的新基礎，整段都在同一權重年內，所以 residual 為 0（資料中 2025-12 為 0.000）。
  2. 四捨五入。BLS 指數公布到小數三位（2007 年前只到一位），官方權重也只有三位小數。
  3. 季節調整（只影響月增率）。各序列的季節因子分別估計，季調後的總體不會剛好等於季調後細項的加權和，量級約 0.001pp。

8.2 精確加總（鏈式分解）

按下儀表板的「精確加總」後，年增率改成逐月串接。先算每一步（相鄰兩個有資料的月份 \(p\to m\)，未季調）的貢獻，並把該步極小的 residual 依權重比例分攤：

$$c_i^{(1)}(m) = RI_i(p)\left(\frac{I_i(m)}{I_i(p)}-1\right) + r^{(1)}(m)\cdot\frac{|RI_i(p)|}{\sum_j |RI_j(p)|}$$

設 \(g_k\) 為第 \(k\) 步的總體變動率（依時間順序 \(k=1,\dots,K\)，從基期之後第一個月到 \(t\)）。由伸縮級數恆等式：

$$\prod_{k=1}^{K}(1+g_k)-1 \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{K} g_k\prod_{j\lt k}(1+g_j)$$

每一步的 \(g_k\) 都能精確拆成 \(\sum_i c_i^{(1)}\)，所以：

$$c_i^{\text{YoY, exact}}(t) \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{K} c_i^{(1)}(k)\prod_{j\lt k}(1+g_j)$$

各項加總剛好等於公布的年增率，而且每一步都落在同一個權重年度內，不受一月換權重影響。意義上，早期月份的貢獻會隨之後的總體漲幅一起複利放大。月增率的精確加總模式，則是把季調 residual 依權重比例分攤。

9. 缺漏月份

2025 年 10 月因聯邦政府停擺，BLS 沒有公布 CPI。所有計算只串接「有資料的月份」：

  • 2025 年 11 月的月增率是 9 月到 11 月的兩個月變動（圖上該柱底部有橘線）。
  • 年增率仍是完整 12 個月的變動；鏈式分解只少了一段串接。
  • 2026 年 10 月的年增率沒有基期指數，屆時依 BLS 的處理方式調整。

1. Goal and notation

The question: "headline CPI rose X%; how many percentage points came from each component?" A component's contribution depends on two things: how big it is in the basket (its weight) and how much its price moved.

3. How the CPI aggregates

The CPI is a modified Laspeyres (Lowe) index: a fixed basket of quantities, repriced each month. Relative to an anchor month \(a\), the all-items index is the weighted mean of the price relatives of any mutually exclusive, exhaustive set of components, weighted by their expenditure shares in the anchor month:

$$\frac{I_{\text{all}}(t)}{I_{\text{all}}(a)} \;=\; \sum_i s_i(a)\,\frac{I_i(t)}{I_i(a)}, \qquad \sum_i s_i(a)=1 \tag{1}$$

with \(s_i(a)=RI_i(a)/100\). Equation (1) holds exactly (up to index rounding) within one weight year. It breaks across a weight update, which is the main source of the residual in section 8.

4. Relative importance

4.1 Within the year

Quantities are fixed but prices move at different rates, so expenditure shares drift. From anchor \(a\) to month \(t\):

$$RI_i(t) \;=\; RI_i(a)\cdot\frac{I_i(t)/I_i(a)}{I_{\text{all}}(t)/I_{\text{all}}(a)} \tag{2}$$

This is BLS's standard price-update formula. Summing (2) over components and using (1) gives 100 again.

4.2 December has two sets of weights

  • Old basis \(RI_i^{\text{old}}(\text{Dec } y)\): weight year \(y\)'s weights, price-updated to December by (2).
  • New basis \(RI_i^{\text{new}}(\text{Dec } y)\): the anchor of weight year \(y+1\). This is the table BLS publishes every December.

Contributions always use base-period weights. When December is the base (January MoM, the following December's YoY), the price change happens inside weight year \(y+1\), so the new basis is the right one. The project sets every December's \(RI\) to the new basis; without that, December contributions do not add up.

5. Where the anchor weights come from

5.1 Official tables (default)

The anchor of weight year \(y\) is BLS's December \(y-1\) table. For each parent → children group, a child's share inside its parent is:

$$s_{i\mid P}(a_y) \;=\; \frac{\widetilde{RI}_i}{\sum_{j\in P}\widetilde{RI}_j}$$

where \(\widetilde{RI}\) is the published value, rounded to three decimals. Dividing by the sum of the children rather than by the parent absorbs the ±0.001pp rounding gap, so the children add up to the parent exactly.

5.2 Recovered from the indexes (fallback)

Used only for weight years with no official table, for example before BLS posts the latest December table. Equation (1) is linear in the unknown \(s_i\). With \(x_{i,t}=I_i(t)/I_i(a)\) and \(b_t=I_P(t)/I_P(a)\), a year gives up to 12 equations, solved as constrained least squares:

$$\min_{s}\ \sum_{t}\Big(\sum_i s_i\,x_{i,t}-b_t\Big)^2 \quad \text{s.t.}\ \sum_i s_i = 1$$

Substituting \(s_n = 1-\sum_{i\lt n}s_i\) turns it into ordinary least squares:

$$\sum_{i\lt n} s_i\,(x_{i,t}-x_{n,t}) \;=\; b_t - x_{n,t}$$

When there are fewer months than unknowns (e.g. only January is out), the previous year's anchor shares are carried forward.

Precision. Before January 2007 BLS published indexes to one decimal. The fit error of (1) rises from about \(3\times10^{-6}\) to about \(5\times10^{-4}\), and because the components' price relatives are highly collinear within a year, food and services cannot be told apart. Recovered weights are within about ±0.05pp of the official ones after 2007 but can be 1–4pp off before. Recovery is therefore only a fallback, and also a check that the official tables were parsed correctly (section 10).

6. Hierarchy and composite components

Weights are built top-down from these mutually exclusive, exhaustive groups: child weight = parent weight × the child's share inside the parent.

ParentChildren
All itemsFood, Energy, Core goods, Core services
All itemsCommodities, Services
FoodFood at home, Food away from home
EnergyEnergy commodities, Energy services
ServicesRent of shelter, Services less rent of shelter

Weights and contributions are linear, so components can be added and subtracted. "Core services ex shelter" is built this way:

$$\text{Core services ex shelter} \;=\; \underbrace{(\text{Services}-\text{Energy services})}_{\text{core services}}-\text{Rent of shelter} \;=\; \text{Services less rent of shelter}-\text{Energy services}$$

7. Contribution formulas

\(RI\) is in %, contributions in percentage points.

7.1 Month over month (seasonally adjusted)

$$c_i^{\text{MoM}}(t) \;=\; RI_i(t-1)\cdot\left(\frac{I^{SA}_i(t)}{I^{SA}_i(t-1)}-1\right)$$

7.2 Year over year (not seasonally adjusted, standard)

$$c_i^{\text{YoY}}(t) \;=\; RI_i(t-12)\cdot\left(\frac{I_i(t)}{I_i(t-12)}-1\right)$$

This is Bloomberg WMA's method and the dashboard default.

7.3 Composite components

For a composite \(C=\sum_k \sigma_k\,k\) with \(\sigma_k=\pm1\), both weight and contribution are signed sums: \(RI_C=\sum_k\sigma_k RI_k\), \(c_C=\sum_k\sigma_k c_k\).

8. The residual and the exact sum

8.1 What the residual is

$$r(t) \;=\; \underbrace{\left(\frac{I_{\text{all}}(t)}{I_{\text{all}}(t-h)}-1\right)\times 100}_{\text{published headline change}} \;-\; \sum_i c_i(t)$$

It is the gap between the sum of the contributions and the published headline. For March 2026: 3.256 − (0.366 + 0.791 + 0.229 + 1.847) = 0.023. It has three sources:

  1. YoY crosses a weight update (the main one). Equation (1) only holds within a weight year. When the base \(t-12\) and \(t\) fall in different weight years, base weights times component changes do not add back to the headline. The counter-example confirms this: December YoY is based on the previous December's new basis, so the whole year sits in one weight year and the residual is 0 (2025-12 shows 0.000).
  2. Rounding. Indexes are published to three decimals (one before 2007) and official weights to three decimals.
  3. Seasonal adjustment (MoM only). Seasonal factors are estimated series by series, so seasonally adjusted headline is not exactly the weighted sum of seasonally adjusted components. The gap is about 0.001pp.

8.2 Exact sum (chained decomposition)

With "Exact sum" on, YoY is built month by month. First each step between consecutive published months \(p\to m\) (NSA) is decomposed and its tiny residual is shared out in proportion to the weights:

$$c_i^{(1)}(m) = RI_i(p)\left(\frac{I_i(m)}{I_i(p)}-1\right) + r^{(1)}(m)\cdot\frac{|RI_i(p)|}{\sum_j |RI_j(p)|}$$

Let \(g_k\) be the headline change of step \(k\), in time order \(k=1,\dots,K\) from the month after the base to \(t\). The telescoping identity

$$\prod_{k=1}^{K}(1+g_k)-1 \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{K} g_k\prod_{j\lt k}(1+g_j)$$

with each \(g_k=\sum_i c_i^{(1)}(k)\) exactly, gives

$$c_i^{\text{YoY, exact}}(t) \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{K} c_i^{(1)}(k)\prod_{j\lt k}(1+g_j)$$

The components add up exactly to the published YoY, and because every step lies inside one weight year the January reset does no harm. Intuitively, an early month's contribution compounds with the headline growth that follows it. For MoM, exact mode shares the seasonal-adjustment residual out in proportion to the weights.

9. Missing months

BLS did not publish the October 2025 CPI because of the federal government shutdown. Every calculation chains over published months only:

  • November 2025 MoM is a two-month change, September to November (orange underline on the chart).
  • YoY is still a true 12-month change; the chained version just has one link fewer.
  • October 2026 YoY has no base index; it will follow whatever BLS does at that point.

11. Limitations and references

  • Relative importance is a not-seasonally-adjusted concept; applying it to seasonally adjusted changes leaves a residual of about 0.001pp in MoM.
  • Before 2007 indexes have one decimal, so the standard YoY residual is about 0.04pp.
  • Until BLS posts the latest December table, that year's weights are recovered; the dashboard footnote says so.
